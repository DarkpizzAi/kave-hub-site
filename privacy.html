kave inbound: privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

kave inbound is a small private tool that one household uses to keep its own travel and household records tidy. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool connects to one Gmail account belonging to the owner. It reads messages that the owner or the owner's household forward to that account (for example booking confirmations), applies labels to those messages, moves them out of the inbox once handled, and sends one summary email a day to the owner's own address. It does not read, label or move any other message, and it never deletes messages.

What it keeps

From the messages it handles, the tool keeps only the facts it needs, such as dates, places and booking references. These are stored in the owner's own Google Drive and in a private code repository that only the owner and household can open. Nothing is published, sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

Automated reading

Some forwarded messages are read by an AI assistant that the owner runs, in order to extract booking details that a simple program cannot. The owner does not use Gmail data to develop, improve or train generalised AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw the tool's access at any time from the Google Account security settings (third-party access), and can delete the stored facts from their own Drive.
